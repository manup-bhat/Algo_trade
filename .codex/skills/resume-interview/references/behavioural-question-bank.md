# Behavioral Question Bank — top 30 + company sets + STAR templates

## STAR(R) template (fill one per story)
- **Situation:** context + why it mattered (1 sentence).
- **Task:** your goal + constraints (scope, deadline, scale).
- **Action:** what **YOU** did, options weighed, **why** you chose your approach.
- **Result:** quantified outcome.
- **(R) Reflection:** what you learned / would do differently.

## Build a 3–5 story bank (each covers many questions)
Pick projects that are **high-impact × high-complexity × high-your-involvement**.
Tag each story with the signals it demonstrates: conflict, ambiguity, failure,
leadership, data-driven, going beyond scope, tight deadline, persuasion.

## Company-flavored examples
- **Amazon:** failed-deadline handling; a mistake and what you learned; acting without
  manager approval; something you'd do differently. (Map to Leadership Principles.)
- **Airbnb:** why Airbnb / mission resonance; a time you were a good "host"; a project
  explained end-to-end; a time you were uncomfortable and how you handled it.
- **ByteDance:** take a product from start to end; biggest achievement; a recent failure.
- **Slack:** your toughest project + its architecture; handling a teammate who wants a
  different language/approach.
- **Palantir:** a strong opinion 90% disagree with; resolving conflict with difficult
  coworkers; an analytical problem you solved.
- **Stripe:** how you stay current; a recent difficult project.

## Model answer skeletons
**"Tell me about yourself" (60–90s):** Present role + scope → 1–2 relevant past wins
(with a metric) → why this role/company now.
**"Conflict" (STAR):** Situation (tension) → Task (resolve + deliver) → Action (talked
to each side, found root cause, proposed a plan, aligned them) → Result (delivered on
time, relationship improved) → Reflection (what you'd do earlier next time).
**"Biggest failure":** Own it plainly → what you learned → the concrete change you made
so it never recurred (shows growth, not defensiveness).

## Practice loop
Write bullet points (not a script) per story → verbalize out loud → have Copilot ask
the question, then ask **2–3 escalating follow-ups** and critique STAR structure,
specificity, and metrics.
